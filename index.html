Academic Work

Research Interests

🌀
Tropical Cyclone Climatology, Bay of Bengal
Investigating environmental threshold conditions for cyclogenesis over the BoB during 1990–2020 using ERA5 reanalysis data. Focus on thermodynamic and dynamic parameter variability across bimodal cyclone seasons.
📊
Climate Data Analysis & Visualization
Advanced GrADS scripting on Ubuntu Linux for overlay plots of SST, Relative Humidity, Vorticity, Wind Shear, and Moisture Flux. ERA5 data at 0.25°×0.25° resolution across domain 70°E–100°E, 5°N–25°N.
🔬
Environmental Threshold Analysis
Quantifying critical thresholds of Sea Surface Temperature, 850 hPa convergence/divergence, and vertical wind shear (850–200 hPa) that govern tropical cyclogenesis events over the Bay of Bengal.
🤝
Collaborative Research — BUET Atmospheric Physics Lab
Collaborated with lab members to develop innovative techniques for climate data analysis and predictive modeling. Investigated the impact of environmental factors on cyclone intensity and track patterns.
